JARVIS SRB

Everything you need to know.

Answers below reflect the current bot configuration and payment flow.

What is JARVIS SRB?

A Telegram bot for supported restricted-content and media workflows, with plan-based limits and additional tools.

How do I start?

Open the Telegram bot and use /start. Available commands and actions are shown by the bot.

What are the subscription plans?

Basic, Standard, Premium, Ultimate and Elite are configured, each with weekly, monthly and yearly prices in INR and USD.

How do I purchase Premium?

You can buy directly from this website: open Pricing, choose a plan and duration, and pay securely through Razorpay. You can also use /buypremium in Telegram, choose a plan and duration, and tap Pay Here.

How is Premium activated?

For payments made inside Telegram, Premium activates automatically after Razorpay payment verification. For website purchases, you receive a unique redeem code after successful payment. Send /redeempremium YOUR-CODE to the Telegram bot to activate the purchased plan.

Can I use a redeem code more than once?

No. Each website purchase code is unique and can be redeemed only once, by one Telegram account. Keep the code safe until you redeem it.

What if payment succeeds but Premium is not activated?

Check /myplan first. If access is still missing, contact support before paying again.

How do referrals work?

A valid new-user referral adds 2 trial uses to the referrer's account. Referral rewards do not change Premium plans.
