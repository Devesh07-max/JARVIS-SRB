JARVIS SRB

How information is handled.

The bot processes Telegram account identifiers and limited profile information needed to register users, manage trial usage, referrals, plan access and premium expiry. Payment processing is handled through Razorpay; payment order identifiers, plan, duration, currency, amount and payment status are stored to verify transactions and activate access. The project also uses MongoDB for application records. Do not send passwords, API keys or payment card details to support. The owner must review and complete the operator contact details, retention periods, applicable legal basis and any additional disclosures before publication.
