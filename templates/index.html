FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is JARVIS SRB free to use?

How can I get Premium?

Use /buypremium in the Telegram bot, select a plan and duration, then open the checkout link generated by the bot.

How is Premium activated?

The backend verifies payment details and captured status. After successful verification, the selected plan is activated automatically.

What if payment succeeds but access is not updated?

Check /myplan in Telegram. If the issue remains, contact the support team using the details on the Contact page.
